Privacy

Short version: I am one person walking dogs in Haarlem. I collect the least I can, I do not sell anything to anyone, and you can ask me to delete your details at any time.

What I collect, and why

When you request an introduction session: your name, email, phone number if you give one, your dog’s details and the area you live in. I use this only to get back to you and arrange a time. It arrives in my email inbox.

When you become a client: at the introduction session I complete a form with your contact details, your dog’s health and behaviour information, your vet’s details, an emergency contact and how keys are handled. I need this to look after your dog safely. It is stored in a private Google Sheet that only I can see, and you get a copy of your own answers by email.

Photos: I send you a photo after every walk. I will only use a photo of your dog anywhere else — on this website or social media — if you have given me permission, and you can withdraw that at any time.

This website

No cookies and no advertising trackers. I use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without storing anything that identifies you personally, so there is no cookie banner to click.

The availability calendar is embedded from Google Calendar and shows only free/busy blocks — no client names, addresses or notes are ever published on it.

How long I keep things

If you enquire and we do not go ahead, I delete your details. If you become a client, I keep your records while I am walking your dog and delete them when you stop, unless I have to keep something for tax records.

Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email walkiesnco@gmail.com and I will sort it out.
