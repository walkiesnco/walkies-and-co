What this page will need to cover

  • The owner stays responsible for their dog, and confirms a valid WA (liability) policy
  • Acknowledgement of natural causes, sudden illness, and age or breed-related risks
  • The owner’s disclosure about their dog is accurate and complete
  • Disclosure of any health issues, aggression or escape history
  • Lead on at all times in public
  • Reasonable care taken, but not liability for events outside my control
  • Emergency vet authorisation, with the owner covering the costs
  • Weather and illness (force majeure)
  • Cancellation and payment
  • Key handling
  • The right to refuse or stop a walk that is not safe
  • Data and privacy

Ground rules for the draft: Dutch governing law; a “reasonable care” standard rather than an attempt to exclude all liability; and no attempt to exclude liability for intent, gross negligence, death or personal injury — an over-broad clause of that kind can simply be set aside under Dutch consumer law, which would leave things worse than a narrower one.
