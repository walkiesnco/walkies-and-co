Meet your walker

Hi, I’m Jean

I grew up on a farm in South Africa and moved to the Netherlands about three years ago. I now live in Haarlem, and I love it here: the canals, the parks and the dunes nearby.

I’ve had a soft spot for dogs my whole life. I grew up with a Weimaraner called Sabrina, so I know how much energy, personality and affection a dog can have, and how much a good walk matters.

I lead an active lifestyle, so time outside with your dog is the best part of my day. I work in business consulting with a flexible schedule, which lets me offer reliable, regular slots, and I cycle to you.

Walkies & Co. is new, and I’m building it the way I’d want my own dog looked after: we meet first, so I know your dog and your routine; your dog is always on a lead; and you get a photo and a short note after every walk.

I’m also learning Dutch, so don’t be surprised if I practise a word or two!

Where I walk

Haarlem, and the bits around it

I cycle to you, so I cover Haarlem – Centrum, Noord, Oost, Zuid and Schalkwijk – along with the areas nearby. If you’re just outside, do ask anyway; there’s a good chance I can still reach you. When we meet, we can talk through which routes you’d like me to take around your area.

Questions

The things people ask

Can I book a walk straight from the website?

No, and that’s on purpose. Every dog starts with a free introduction so I can meet you both first. Once we’ve met and agreed a routine, I put your walks in the diary myself. The calendar here shows when I’m free, but nothing is reserved until we’ve actually spoken.

What happens at the introduction?

About 15 minutes, free, at your place. We meet, I meet your dog, and we go through vet details, health, habits, what sets them off, keys and routes. If it feels right for all three of us, we agree your slots there and then.

What if the weather is bad?

I don’t walk in heat, thunderstorms or ice. Hot pavement does real damage to paws, and a storm frightens even confident dogs. If I have to skip a walk I’ll message you, and you aren’t charged.

How do I pay?

We’ll sort that out at the introduction – it’s straightforward, and there’s nothing to set up in advance.

Do you take reactive or nervous dogs?

I’m starting with friendly, healthy, lead-trained dogs. If your dog has a bite history or serious reactivity, I’d rather say honestly that it isn’t the right fit yet than take a risk with them. Do ask, though – nervous isn’t the same as reactive, and a lot of shy dogs do brilliantly once the routine is predictable.

Do you speak Dutch?

I’m learning! I work in English, which suits plenty of people here, and I’m always happy to practise a few words with you.

What if I need to cancel?

Life happens, just let me know. More than 12 hours ahead, there’s no charge at all. Under 12 hours it’s half the walk price, because by then the slot has usually gone.
